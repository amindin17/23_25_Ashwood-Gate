About Us

The Ashwood Gait Residents' Committee represents the interests of residents at 23 & 25 Ashwood Gait. We work with residents, factors, and other stakeholders to help maintain and improve our community, address shared concerns, and promote a positive living environment for everyone.

Our Values

Community

We encourage a friendly, respectful, and inclusive neighbourhood.

Transparency

We communicate openly and share relevant information with residents.

Representation

We listen to residents' views and seek to represent them fairly.

Collaboration

We work constructively with residents, property managers, and local organisations.

Responsibility

We aim to improve our development for the benefit of current and future residents.
